How the reports were split

Every report body was read, not just its subject or its intake tag.

  • parser. The shared parser reads dates month first and every tenant on file writes the day first, so day and month swap. Placed by tenant, as causes.ts does.
  • hint. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and reads the date day first. People type what the hint says. Only the form channel of booking-v3 tenants.
  • one-day. The date moves by one day, or differs between attendees in different timezones. A day/month swap keeps both numbers and cannot do that. These are the reports the desk already read as one-day moves (desk-notes.json). The desk counts them where they are filed, under parser by tenant, and they get a ticket of their own.

Why one ticket hid three

Decision 0001 merges on the subject line, and the triage page says to merge when unsure. Three problems with the same symptom got the same subjects. Intake tags did not help: intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and the cause follows the tenant.

The count of a merged ticket is not the count of one bug. Before a ticket is marked known, split it by cause and check each cause against the bodies.

What happened to the tickets

  • #2 hint won’t be fixed: the booking-v3 form is retired. Closed as not planned.
  • #3 one-day is closed as a duplicate of #1. The desk counts its reports where they are filed, under parser by tenant, so they are already in #1’s count.
